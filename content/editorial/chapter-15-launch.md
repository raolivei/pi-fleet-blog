“Add the missing pools” sounds like one feature.

In SwimTO, it meant discovery, location identifiers, schedule matching, a registry and recurring checks. Starting a PR wouldn't make those decisions disappear.

The epic kept the larger goal. A child issue defined the discovery work. The implementation PR said: “Closes #179, advances #178 (Phase 1).”

That distinction matters. One part could be merged while the larger effort remained open.

The record wasn't perfect either. That PR included mobile fixes, and some verification checks were still unchecked. Linking the work made those limits visible; it didn't finish the work for me.

I wrote about how I want to use that trail more consistently across sessions and AI assistants: the goal, the next testable piece, the implementation and the result of checking it.
